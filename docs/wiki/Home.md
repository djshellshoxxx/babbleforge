# BabbleForge Wiki

BabbleForge is a desktop speech-masking generator: stationary speech-shaped noise, multi-talker babble and hybrids,
at a controlled digital level and across multichannel layouts. V1 is a statistically controlled, reproducible masker with
no microphone. V2 (planned) adds room analysis and calibration with a measurement microphone.
